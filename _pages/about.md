My research spans the analysis, optimal operation, and market mechanisms for modern power systems, with particular emphasis on coordinated optimization across multiple system areas and layers. Detailed topics include multi-area coordinated dispatch and electricity trading, transmission–distribution–microgrid coordination, DER aggregation, and multi-energy flow integration. I have developed an equivalent projection theory for optimization models, as well as a non-iterative coordinated optimization method for hierarchical systems. Currently, we are extending these foundations toward AI agent-based coordination and the engineering deployment in real-world practice.

I received my Ph.D. and B.E. degrees in Electrical Engineering from Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Qing Xia and Prof. Haiwang Zhong. I also hold a dual degree in Economics from Tsinghua University.
